## Sam

Sam wants a number for whether more cores helped. The number is the efficiency, `T(1) / (N * T(N))`, next to the speedup `T(1) / T(N)`. In the worked series, 8 cores take 1.6 seconds where 1 core takes 8.0. The speedup is 5 and the efficiency is 0.625. The cores helped. Each added core contributed less than the one before it. A shorter `real` line means less time waiting. The efficiency says how much of the extra machine showed up in that wait.
