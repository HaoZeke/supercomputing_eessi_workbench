# Provenance

The files under `episodes/files/hands-on/` are copied from
`course-module-snellius-hybrid-jobs` on branch `src/eessi-notes` at
`ccc6fe21e71ea5d46a34488461c0008987de084f`.

That module was adapted from *Introducción a la Supercomputación* by
José Luis Bosque, Borja Pérez, Ramón Beivide, and Carmen Martínez of the
Universidad de Cantabria, by the EuroCC NL team at SURF. The license is
still open. See the TODO(Rohit) callout in `index.md`.

`episodes/files/solutions/matmul-omp.c` is new. It adds an OpenMP pragma
on the outer loop of `hands-on/ex5/matmul.c`. The checksum printf is
unchanged: `Matrix checksum: %.17g`.
